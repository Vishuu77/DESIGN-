# Supplier Shop-Floor Technical Assessment & Audit Checklist
### **Venttup Ventures Private Limited &bull; Lead Auditor: Er. Vishnu Ramachandra**

Used for physical shop-floor qualification across tier-1 and tier-2 precision engineering suppliers in Bengaluru and Hyderabad.

## 1. Machine Tool Capability & Calibration
- [ ] CNC Machining Centers (3-axis, 4-axis, 5-axis) have active annual calibration certificates (ISO 230-2).
- [ ] Laser cutting machines (Fiber ≥ 3kW) maintain kerf tolerance within ±0.05 mm on 1.6mm - 6.0mm plate.
- [ ] CNC Hydraulic Press Brakes (Amada / Trumpf / equivalent) equipped with multi-axis backgauges and laser angle measurement.

## 2. Metrology & Quality Control Lab
- [ ] Dedicated temperature-controlled inspection room maintained at 20°C ± 2°C.
- [ ] Coordinate Measuring Machine (CMM) calibrated and certified within 12-month interval.
- [ ] Surface roughness tester (Mitutoyo Surftest) available for Ra verification down to 0.4 µm.
- [ ] Height masters, granite comparator tables (Grade 00), and thread ring/plug gauges indexed in calibration registers.

## 3. Fabrication & Welding Standards
- [ ] Qualified Welding Procedure Specification (WPS) and Procedure Qualification Record (PQR) per ASME Section IX / AWS D1.1.
- [ ] Certified TIG/MIG welders with valid Welder Performance Qualifications (WPQ).
- [ ] Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) capabilities: Dye Penetrant Inspection (DPI) and Magnetic Particle Testing (MPT).

## 4. Surface Treatment & Finishing
- [ ] 7-tank or 9-tank pre-treatment chemical bath system for powder coating with daily titration logs.
- [ ] Cross-hatch adhesion testing (ASTM D3359 Method B) achieving 5B rating.
- [ ] Dry Film Thickness (DFT) gauge calibrated to verify 70-90 µm uniform coating thickness.
- [ ] Salt spray testing chamber (ASTM B117) certification achieving ≥ 500 hours without red rust.

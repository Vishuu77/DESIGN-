# GD&T Standards Matrix (ASME Y14.5M & ISO 1101)
### **Authored by Er. Vishnu Ramachandra &bull; Venttup Ventures Private Limited**

This matrix governs tolerance allocation across domestic Indian suppliers (Peenya, Bommasandra, and Hosur clusters) for precision CNC machining, sheet-metal bending, and welded skid fabrications.

| Symbol | Characteristic | Typical Tolerance (CNC Turning) | Typical Tolerance (CNC Milling) | Typical Tolerance (Sheet Metal) | Inspection Equipment |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **⏤** | Straightness | 0.015 mm | 0.020 mm | 0.20 mm | Dial Indicator / Granite Table |
| **⏥** | Flatness | 0.020 mm | 0.025 mm | 0.35 mm | CMM / 3-Point Probe |
| **○** | Circularity (Roundness) | 0.010 mm | 0.018 mm | N/A | V-Block & Dial Gauge |
| **⌭** | Cylindricity | 0.012 mm | N/A | N/A | Coordinate Measuring Machine |
| **∥** | Parallelism | 0.025 mm | 0.030 mm | 0.40 mm | Height Gauge / CMM |
| **⟂** | Perpendicularity | 0.020 mm | 0.025 mm | 0.30 mm | Precision Square & Indicator |
| **∠** | Angularity | ±0° 15' | ±0° 20' | ±0° 45' | Optical Bevel Protractor |
| **⌖** | Position (True Position) | Ø 0.030 mm (MMC) | Ø 0.040 mm (MMC) | Ø 0.25 mm (RFS) | Automated Vision CMM |
| **◎** | Concentricity | 0.020 mm | 0.025 mm | N/A | Dual Indicator Mandrel |
| **↗** | Circular Runout | 0.015 mm | 0.025 mm | N/A | Center Lathe & Dial Test Indicator |

### Process Capability Guidance (Cpk Targets)
- **Critical Features (Bearings, Gaskets, Shaft journals):** Cpk ≥ 1.33 (32 ppm defect ceiling)
- **Standard Sourcing Features (Mounting brackets, fastener clearances):** Cpk ≥ 1.10
